Core identity

An elite student community with a real company behind it.

GWD Club is a student-driven chapter based in Hyderabad. It operates under its parent firm, GWD Global Pvt Ltd, which puts members beside live business work: automation projects, sports broadcasts and startup conversations.

The club exists to change a familiar pattern. Students collect marks and certificates, then learn the job after graduation. Here they start on the job while they study.

Shell: GWD Global · Core: Club · Orbit: members

Real-world execution · Bootcamp

The GWD AI Automation Bootcamp.

A hands-on build programme where members wire real tools into working automations. The stack is n8n, OpenAI, Google APIs and Apify. Each tool owns one stage of the pipeline.

Members leave with workflows they built, tested and can show, not slides about them.

n8nOpenAIGoogle APIsApify

Hackathons and build sprints

Real-world execution · On the ground

Live matchdays, run by student interns.

Through GWD Sports, the club's interns work inside a major tournament, the Hyderabad Super League. They run the workflows that keep a matchday on schedule, operate the live stream and film the game.

It is the same standard a paid crew works to, with students holding the controls.

Operations

Matchday workflow management

Kick-off timings, team sheets, crew positions and the handoffs between them.

Broadcast

OBS live streaming

Scenes, overlays, camera and audio feeds, and the switching between them while the match is live.
